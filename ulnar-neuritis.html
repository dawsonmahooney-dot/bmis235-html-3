Ulnar Neuritis

The ulnar nerve, the "funny bone" nerve, runs through a groove behind the inside of the elbow. The bending and stretching of throwing can pull on it, squeeze it or make it slide out of place, which irritates the nerve.

Signs include numbness or tingling in the ring and little fingers, pain along the inner elbow and a weaker grip. Treatment usually starts with rest and avoiding keeping the elbow bent for long periods. If symptoms keep coming back, surgery can move the nerve to a safer spot.
