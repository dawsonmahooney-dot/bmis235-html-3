Flexor Pronator Strain

The flexor pronator muscles start at the bony bump on the inside of the elbow and run down the forearm. They bend the wrist, grip the ball and turn the forearm, and they help protect the UCL when you throw.

Overuse can strain these muscles or irritate their tendon. It shows up as soreness just below the inner elbow that gets worse when gripping or snapping the wrist. Most players get better with rest, ice and a forearm strengthening program before a gradual return to throwing.
